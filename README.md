# SAT Vocab Practice
A calm, no-account practice area for the 384 words in [PrepScholar's SAT vocabulary list](https://blog.prepscholar.com/sat-vocabulary-words): 39 units of up to 10 words, a fill-in-the-blank review of only the two units just finished (or the last unit alone if it has no partner) and then a cumulative review (Unit 1 through the current unit) after every two units, six 60-word Big Tests, and a 384-word Final Test; a Daily Review tab (each Pacific day: Part 1 checks meanings for all previously started words; after 100% you return to the Daily Review tab, where a separate Part 2: Fill in the blank section unlocks for a random half of those words; both parts must be 100% on the first try before the path continues; ungraded study cards have Previous, and unit quizzes have Review words so you can peek at the unit list and return to the same question without changing your score; from Unit 9 on, an optional Recent Words Recall typing practice on the Daily Review tab focuses on recently learned words and does not gate the path); a Grammar Rules path of 28 topics drawn from PrepScholar's SAT Grammar Rules You Must Know (Part 1: the 9 must-know rules in that guide's order, then a 60-question exam) and The Complete Guide to SAT Grammar Rules (the remaining topics, with a review after every 5 rules and a final exam on all 28); a daily grammar review on the Daily Review tab of rules already passed, required before new grammar that day but not gating the vocab path; each step unlocks after 100% first-try accuracy on the previous one, flashcards, multiple choice, typed recall, spaced reviews (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days), weak-word practice, and backup/restore. Progress is stored in your browser's localStorage.

Definitions come from the PrepScholar list (see source-prepscholar.json), as are the example sentences. Not affiliated with College Board. Run tests with `node test.js`.
