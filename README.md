# SAT Vocab Practice
A calm, no-account practice area for the 384 words in [PrepScholar's SAT vocabulary list](https://blog.prepscholar.com/sat-vocabulary-words): 39 units of up to 10 words, a fill-in-the-blank review of only the two units just finished (or the last unit alone if it has no partner) six 60-word Big Tests, and a 384-word Final Test; a Daily Review tab (every other Pacific calendar day: Part 1 checks meanings for all previously started words; after 90% or higher you return to the Daily Review tab, where a separate Part 2: Fill in the blank section unlocks for a random half of those words; both parts must be 90% or higher on the first try before the path continues; ungraded study cards have Previous, and unit quizzes have Review words so you can peek at the unit list and return to the same question without changing your score; from Unit 9 on, an optional Recent Words Recall typing practice on the Daily Review tab focuses on recently learned words and does not gate the path); a Grammar Rules path of 28 topics drawn from PrepScholar's SAT Grammar Rules You Must Know (Part 1: the 9 must-know rules in that guide's order, then a 60-question exam) and The Complete Guide to SAT Grammar Rules (the remaining topics, with a review after every 5 rules and a final exam on all 28); a daily grammar review on the Daily Review tab of rules already passed, required before new grammar that day but not gating the vocab path; each step unlocks after 90% or higher first-try accuracy on the previous one, flashcards, multiple choice, typed recall, optional Practice words (Choose your practice words at the top of the path: pick any words you have already started, then typing, fill-in-the-blank, or multiple choice, with at least one question per word; recent practice sets are saved automatically and can be reselected from the bottom of that screen; in-progress quizzes in every mode can be resumed after you leave or close the tab, or restarted without erasing progress or unlocks), an Extra Words tab (light, ungated daily practice on a larger unofficial list of 1,308 headwords: the SparkNotes “1000 Most Common SAT Words” selection plus SesameWords Top Frequency SAT words that were not already on that list; sessions of 5–20 words, default 12, weighted toward top-frequency, unseen, and missed words; each word is shown with a definition and a sentence, then a short question; misses simply return later; words seen and words known are stored separately from Path unlocks); a Profile tab (your name, daily word and time goals, a first-visit survey you can retake anytime, active study time, a projected finish date, a choice of 10 study buddies, and an optional photo), a friendly study buddy (frog by default), and backup/restore. Progress is stored in your browser's localStorage.

Path definitions and example sentences come from the PrepScholar list (see source-prepscholar.json). Extra Words definitions are from Princeton WordNet 3.0 or are original; Extra Words example sentences are original or from WordNet. SparkNotes and SesameWords wording is not copied. Not affiliated with College Board. Run tests with `node test.js`.
